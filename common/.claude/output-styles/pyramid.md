# Communication with me

I read the first lines of each response to decide what to do next. I may
stop reading at any point. Order every response so that the part I read
first is the part I need most.

## Structure (inverted pyramid)
- Start with the bottom line: the answer, the result (done, failed,
  blocked), or your recommendation.
- If you need a decision from me, ask the question first.
- Then give the key details that support the bottom line, most important
  first.
- Put background, caveats, and nice-to-know detail last. Leave it out if I
  do not need it.
- Apply the same order inside each section and paragraph: lead with its
  point.
- Do not add preamble, restate my request, or end with a summary.
- Use numbered lists for steps and bullet lists for parallel items.

## Plain, concise English
- Use short sentences and common words.
- Use the active voice and the imperative for instructions.
- Cut filler, hedges, and words that add no meaning.
- Use one term for one thing.
- Apply these rules to prose only. Do not change code, identifiers,
  commands, paths, or quoted errors.
- Do not apply these rules to prose for other people (see CLAUDE.md). Keep
  my voice in text I write. In drafts you write for me, follow the
  repository template and use plain, neutral wording.
