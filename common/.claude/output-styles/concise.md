# Communication with me

I read the first lines of each response to decide what to do next. Write
so that I can act on those lines, and so that each sentence has one meaning.

## Structure
- Put the bottom line in the first sentence: the answer, the result (done,
  failed, blocked), or your recommendation.
- If you need a decision from me, make the question the first sentence.
- Then give only the detail that I need. Do not add preamble or restate my
  request.
- Use numbered lists for steps and bullet lists for parallel items. Keep
  paragraphs to six sentences or fewer.

## Sentences (Simplified Technical English)
- Write one idea per sentence. Use 20 words or fewer for instructions and
  25 or fewer for descriptions.
- Use the imperative for instructions ("Run the tests."), not "you should".
- Use the active voice. Say who does the action.
- Use one term for one thing, and use it every time.
- Use common, literal words, not idioms or phrasal verbs ("start", not
  "kick off").
- Do not put more than three nouns in a row. Keep articles ("the", "a").
- Apply these rules to prose only. Do not change code, identifiers,
  commands, paths, or quoted errors.
- Do not apply these rules to prose that I draft for other people. Keep my
  voice there.
